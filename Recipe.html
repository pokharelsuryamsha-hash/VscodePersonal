Chicken Burger

This quick chicken burger recipe makes juicy, flavorful patties in under 20 minutes. Combine 1 lb ground chicken,1/2 finely grated onion,1/2 cup breadcrumbs, 1 egg, and 2 cloves minced garlic [5.4]. Season with salt and pepper, shape into 4 patties, and pan-fry over medium-high heat for 5-6 minutes per side until golden [5.4].

Ingredients

  • ground chicken
  • egg
  • Buns
  • lettuce
  • tomato

Instructions

  1. Mix: In a bowl, combine chicken, breadcrumbs, egg, onion, garlic, salt, and pepper until just combined.
  2. Shape: Divide mixture into 4 patties (approx \(2\text{cm}\) thick).
  3. Coat (Optional): Dip patties in extra breadcrumbs for a crisper texture.
  4. Cook: Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high. Cook for 5–6 minutes on one side until brown, then flip and cook for 3–4 minutes until fully cooked (\(165^{\circ}\text{F}\) internal).
  5. Assemble: Serve on toasted buns with desired toppings.
